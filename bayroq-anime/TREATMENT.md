# Bayroq tongi — treatment

**Style:** 80s Cel Anime (`cel-anime-80s`). **Topic:** 18 November, State Flag Day of Uzbekistan (flag adopted 18 Nov 1991).
**Presenter:** Diyorbek, 13, pupil of school 54, class 8-E: slim teen build, white shirt, black suit jacket and trousers.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 24 fps, ~62 s, Uzbek subtitles on the tape. Voice: none yet (lip flaps timed to the lines; a voice file can replace the timing later).

## 1. Three structures, and the choice

1. **The morning the flag goes up.** Dawn in the school courtyard. Diyorbek introduces himself, raises the flag; each colour is named as it climbs past; at the top the air is still — then the sun clears the mountains and a gust opens the flag. One place, two colour models (blue dawn → gold sunrise).
2. **Mock opening credits.** A roster of the flag's parts introduced like anime characters, each with a pose and a name card, Diyorbek as host. Fun, but the flag's parts are not characters and the day itself gets lost.
3. **A TV switched on.** An in-world broadcast of the anniversary, Diyorbek watching at home. Tape grammar fits, but he would be a viewer, not the one who acts.

**Chosen: 1.** He wants something (to raise the flag himself on its day), something changes (it reaches the top and hangs limp; then the sun and the wind arrive), it ends differently (the flag open over the waking city). The colour-model change carries the turn; the meanings of the colours land one per beat as the flag climbs.

## 2. Logline and arc

At dawn on 18 November, Diyorbek raises the flag in his school courtyard; at the top it hangs still, until the sun clears the mountains and the wind opens it over the city.
Setup: blue dawn, greeting, title. Rise: the flag climbs, five meanings. Turn: stillness, one beat of silence, the sun's first ray (impact frame). End: gold light, the flag waving, crane up, "Bayramingiz muborak!".

## 3. Benchmark

- **Late-80s OVA openings** (generic, no specific work): *learn* painted dawn plates with an emissive pair, held busts with light bands, the chrome title with a glint, cutting on bars. *Don't take* any character, logo, title design or shot.
- **Sunrise flag-raising ceremonies on TV**: *learn* the low angle up a pole, the slow climb, the moment the flag catches the wind. *Don't take* footage or anthem.

## 5. Sound

City-pop / anime-opening pop at 120 BPM: FM electric piano, slap bass, synth brass, gated snare, analog arpeggio, bell sparkle. A brass hit on the title, a tom fill as the flag climbs, one beat of true silence before the sun, the chorus a semitone higher after it. Foley: tape power-on thunk and whine, footsteps on paving, rope through the pulley (squeak per pull), cloth flutter, morning birds, wind gust at the turn. Mix −14 LUFS.

## 7. Changes made while building

- Lines were retimed so that, with no voice yet, every subtitle stays on screen long enough to read (non-space characters ÷ 15 + 1.6 s).
  The meanings now start as the flag leaves the ground (21.3 s) and the last one (12 stars) runs into the stillness shot.
- The rope shot puts Diyorbek beside the pole, the rope in his hands. The ending cranes up less, so the horizon stays in frame, and the end title sits over the school roof.
