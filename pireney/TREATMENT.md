# Pireney sayohati — treatment

**Style:** 80s Cel Anime (`cel-anime-80s`), the same engine as *Bayroq tongi*. **Topic:** 8-mavzu, "Pireney yarimoroli mamlakatlari:
Ispaniya va Portugaliya" (8th-grade history textbook, pp. 45–49). **Format:** 1920×1080, 24 fps, ~106 s, Uzbek. No voice (the
speech service is not available here): the dialogue is on the tape as subtitles with the speaker's name, and the speaker's lips move.

## Story

A father and his son drive along a country road. The son, reading his history book, asks: "Ota, qirol Ferdinand kim bo'lgan?"
The father begins to explain — the book glows, the tape tears, and the two of them land on a hill in medieval Granada.
The father keeps talking, and every place he names is where they now stand: the Alhambra, the court of Alfonso X with its
armillary sphere, the wedding of Ferdinand and Isabella, the harbour of Lisbon with the caravels, the court of Amir Temur where
Clavijo presents his letter. A painted map shows the kingdoms joining into Spain. Back in the car: "Ha, ota, tushundim!" — and they
drive on into the sunset.

## Three structures, and the choice

1. **The trip into the book** (chosen): the car → the past → the car. A bookended journey; every fact is a place they are standing in.
2. **A lecture on a map**: a single map that fills in while the father talks. Clear, but no people, no country, and the user asked to see them.
3. **The son dreams it**: he falls asleep in the car. Weaker: the user asked that they *both* fall in, and that the father keep explaining.

## Facts (from the textbook pages)

711–714 Arab conquest; Granada emirate 1238–1492, the last Muslim state in Western Europe, the Alhambra (Al-Hamro); Castile the
largest kingdom, cortes first called 1137; Alfonso X: "Huquqning yetti bo'limi", Toledo translation school, observatory; Navarra
905–1512, capital Pamplona; 1469 Ferdinand of Aragon marries Isabella of Castile → Spain; 1492 Granada, 1512 Navarra annexed;
Portugal: 1139 Afonso Henriques first king, Lisbon capital; Vasco da Gama reaches India 1498, Pedro Cabral Brazil 1500;
King Henry III of Castile sends embassies to Amir Temur (1402; 1403 Clavijo), Clavijo's "Esdaliklar".

## Sound

An adventure score in the same synth engine: Spanish colour (Phrygian dominant, nylon-guitar arpeggios, castanet clicks),
a brass hit at the jump, softer under the dialogue, a lift for Samarkand, the opening theme returning in the car. Foley: engine
hum, tyres, the jump (whoosh, tape tear), market murmur in Granada, court ambience, gulls and water in Lisbon, wind.
